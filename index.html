Our Story

Born in the
small hours

EMBER began in a cramped garage at 3 AM, fueled by obsession and a single espresso shot. We believe the best coffee happens when the world is quiet — when every variable can be controlled, every bean given full attention.

We source single-origin beans from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra. Each batch is roasted in micro-lots under the cover of darkness, locking in flavors that most never get to taste.
